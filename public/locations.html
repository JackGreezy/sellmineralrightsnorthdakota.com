County first

The record trail starts where the minerals are located.

Tract specific

Basin context never replaces the legal description.

Checks reconciled

Revenue is tied to wells, products, decimals, and dates.

Future separated

Offsets and permits remain distinct from current production.

Terms visible

The complete conveyance and closing conditions sit beside price.

Questions routed

Legal, tax, title, appraisal, and exchange issues go to qualified professionals.
